= Create, publish, and promote a content view

[role="_abstract"]
A _content view_ is a curated snapshot of your repositories.
When you publish a content view, {Project} takes a point-in-time copy of the selected repositories.
You then promote that version through lifecycle environments.

In this step, you create a content view, publish the first version, and promote it to Dev.

.Prerequisites
* You have synchronized at least one repository.
* You have created a lifecycle environment path.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Lifecycle* > *Content Views*.
. Click *Create content view*.
. In the *Name* field, enter a name, for example `My Content View`.
. Ensure *Content view* is selected as the type.
. Click *Create content view*.
. On the *Repositories* tab, select the repository type from the *Type* list, select the checkboxes next to the repositories you want to include, and click *Add repositories*.
. Click *Publish new version*.
. Optional: In the *Description* field, enter `Initial version`.
. Click *Promote* and select the *Dev* lifecycle environment.
. Click *Next*, review the details, and click *Finish*.

Your content view version 1.0 is now published and promoted to the Dev environment.
Hosts registered to Dev with this content view will receive exactly the packages from this snapshot.

In the next step, you create an activation key that maps new hosts to this content view and lifecycle environment.
