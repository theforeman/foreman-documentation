= Content flow in {Project}

[role="_abstract"]
Content in {ProjectName} flows from external sources through {ProjectServer} and {SmartProxyServers} to reach hosts.

The content flow follows these stages in {Project}:

. *Sourcing*: {ProjectServer} synchronizes content from external sources such as the Red{nbsp}Hat Customer Portal,
ifdef::satellite[]
custom Yum repositories,
endif::[]
ifndef::satellite[]
custom Deb and Yum repositories, PyPI,
endif::[]
Git repositories, Ansible collections, container registries, SCAP repositories, and internal data stores.

. *Organization*: On {ProjectServer}, you organize the synchronized content into content views and promote them through lifecycle environments.
With content views, you can curate a specific subset of content versions, while lifecycle environments represent stages such as Development, Testing, and Production.

. *Distribution*: {SmartProxyServers} mirror the content that {ProjectServer} has organized.
Each host retrieves content from its assigned {SmartProxyServer} or, for hosts without a {SmartProxy}, directly from {ProjectServer}.
