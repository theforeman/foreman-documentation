= Organize {Project} resources for management

[role="_abstract"]
Organize {Project} resources including hosts, virtual machines, organizations, and locations into a logical structure to manage them effectively across your infrastructure.

{Project} provides organizational capabilities for structuring your infrastructure according to business units, geographic locations, and lifecycle stages:

* *Organizations* define business unit boundaries for content, permissions, and settings governance
* *Locations* define geographic or physical boundaries for network, subnet, and domain governance
* *Content views* control which software versions hosts can consume based on their lifecycle stage
* *Virtual machine associations* bring existing VMs under central management without recreating them

Use these organizational capabilities together to ensure hosts receive appropriate content and settings based on their business context.
Start by associating VMs from hypervisors, assign hosts to appropriate organizations and locations, then configure content view environments to control software delivery.

Common organizational workflows include:

Onboarding existing infrastructure:: Associate all VMs from a compute resource at once to bring existing infrastructure under central management, then assign to organizations and locations based on business unit and geographic structure.

Reorganizing by business unit:: When hosts need to move between departments, reassign them to different organizations using the "Fix on mismatch" option to automatically add required resources like domains and subnets.

Managing multi-region infrastructure:: Assign hosts to locations representing geographic regions or data centers, ensuring each location has appropriate subnets and domains for local network configuration.

Controlling software lifecycle:: Change content view environments to move hosts between lifecycle stages such as Development, Testing, and Production, controlling which software versions each host can install.

Decommissioning from central management:: Disassociate VMs from {Project} when they no longer need central management, safely removing them from inventory without deleting the VMs from their hypervisors.
