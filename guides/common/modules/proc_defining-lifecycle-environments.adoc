= Define lifecycle environments

[role="_abstract"]
Lifecycle environments represent stages in your content promotion pipeline.
Every {Project} deployment starts with a _Library_ environment that holds all synchronized content.
From Library, you promote tested content through environments you define.

In this step, you create a simple path: *Library > Dev > Prod*.

.Prerequisites
* You have synchronized at least one repository.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Lifecycle* > *Lifecycle Environments*.
. Click *New Environment Path* to start a new lifecycle environment path.
. In the *Name* field, enter `Dev`.
. Optional: In the *Description* field, enter a description, for example `Development and testing`.
. Click *Save*.
. To extend the path, click *Add New Environment* next to the *Dev* environment.
. In the *Name* field, enter `Prod`.
. Ensure the *Prior Environment* is set to *Dev*.
. Click *Save*.

You now have a lifecycle environment path: Library > Dev > Prod.
In the next step, you create a content view to control which repositories are available in these environments.
