= Delete a host on {opentofu-target}

[role="_abstract"]
You can delete hosts running on {opentofu-target} through {Project}.

include::snip_warning-destroy-vm-on-host-delete.adoc[]

.Prerequisites
* Ensure that the `destroy_vm_on_host_delete` setting is enabled.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Hosts* > *All Hosts*.
. Select your host.
. From the actions menu, click *Delete*.
. Click *Delete* to delete your host from {Project} and {opentofu-target}.
