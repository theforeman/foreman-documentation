= Synchronize content between {Project} Servers in air-gapped environments

[role="_abstract"]
Transfer content from an upstream {ProjectServer} connected to the internet to a downstream {ProjectServer} that is completely isolated from all external networks by physically exporting and importing content.

In air-gapped deployments, your downstream {ProjectServer} has no network connection to the upstream {ProjectServer}.
Use {ISS} with export and import to synchronize the Library environment, content view versions, or individual repositories between servers.

The workflow involves exporting content on the upstream {ProjectServer}, physically transferring the export to the air-gapped downstream {ProjectServer}, and importing the content.
You can export content incrementally to minimize the size of subsequent transfers.
