= Recover from failed Ansible content imports by using {ProjectWebUI}

[role="_abstract"]
If you try to import Ansible content to {Project} and the import workflow fails, you can recover database inconsistencies.
The consistency check ensures that your {Project} database only contains properly imported Ansible content.

.Prerequisites
* Your {Project} account has a role that grants the `destroy_ansible_content`, `view_ansible_content`, and `view_organizations` permissions.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Ansible* > *Content*.
. Click *Perform consistency check*.
. Click *Confirm* to perform a consistency check on {Project} to recover from failed Ansible content imports.

.Verification
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Monitor* > *{Project} Tasks* > *Tasks*.
. Search for `ForemanAnsibleDirector::Actions::ConsistencyCheck::Perform`.
. Verify that the task is in state *stopped* with result *Success*.

.Next steps
* If your Ansible content import failed due to network issues, you can retry to import Ansible content.
