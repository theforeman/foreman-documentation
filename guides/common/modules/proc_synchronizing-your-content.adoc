= Synchronizing your content

[role="_abstract"]
Synchronization downloads metadata and packages from an upstream source into {Project}.
Until you synchronize a repository, {Project} has no content to distribute to your hosts.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Products*.
. Select the product that contains the repositories you want to synchronize.
. Select the checkboxes next to the repositories you want to synchronize.
. Click *Sync Now*.
+
Depending on the repository size and your network speed, the sync can take from a few minutes to over an hour.
The first time you synchronized a repository, the sync typically takes longer.

.Verification
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Sync Status*.
. Expand the product to see the synchronization result for each repository.
. Verify that the synchronization completed successfully and that the repository shows a package count.

.Next steps
* After the sync finishes, your content is stored in the _Library_ environment.
Library is the default lifecycle environment where all content lands first.
Next, follow xref:defining-lifecycle-environments[] to create additional lifecycle environments to stage content through a promotion path.
