= Using custom SSL certificate for hosts

[role="_abstract"]
You can use a custom SSL certificate on your hosts to enable encrypted communications between {ProjectServer}, {SmartProxyServer}, and hosts.
Before deploying a custom SSL certificate to your hosts, ensure that you have configured it on your {ProjectServer}.

To reset the custom SSL certificate on your hosts to the default self-signed certificate, re-register your hosts through global registration.
