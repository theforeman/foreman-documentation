= Enable the Templates plugin

[role="_abstract"]
You can enable the Templates plugin to synchronize provisioning, report, and other templates between your {ProjectServer} and a version control system or local directory.
This helps manage templates in Git repositories or keep them synchronized across {Project} instances.

.Procedure
. Enable the plugin on your {ProjectServer}:
+
[options="nowrap", subs="+quotes,verbatim,attributes"]
----
# {foreman-installer} --enable-foreman-plugin-templates
----
. Optional: Enable the Hammer CLI plugin on your {ProjectServer}:
+
[options="nowrap", subs="+quotes,verbatim,attributes"]
----
# {foreman-installer} --enable-foreman-cli-templates
----

.Verification
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Administer* > *About*.
. On the *Plugins* tab, ensure that the page includes the *foreman_templates* plugin.

.Next steps
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Administer* > *Settings* > *Template Sync* to configure the plugin.
For more information, see xref:template-sync-settings[].
