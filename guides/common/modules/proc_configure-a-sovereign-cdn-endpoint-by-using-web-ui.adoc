= Configure a sovereign CDN endpoint by using {ProjectWebUI}

[role="_abstract"]
Configure your {ProjectServer} to synchronize Red{nbsp}Hat content from a sovereign CDN endpoint instead of the default `cdn.redhat.com` endpoint.
Use this to ensure that content synchronization uses an endpoint in your preferred jurisdiction.

.Prerequisites
* You have uploaded a manifest to your organization.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Subscriptions*.
. Click *Manage Manifest*.
. Select the *CDN Configuration* tab.
. Select the *Custom CDN* tab.
. In the *URL* field, enter the URL of a sovereign CDN endpoint:
+
* `\https://cdn-eu.redhat.com` for the European Union
* `\https://cdn-us.redhat.com` for the United States
. Set the *SSL CA Content Credential* field to *N/A*.
. Click *Update*.
