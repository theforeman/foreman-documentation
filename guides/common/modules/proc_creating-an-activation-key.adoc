= Create an activation key

[role="_abstract"]
An _activation key_ assigns a lifecycle environment, content view, and other settings to hosts during registration.
You define the mappings once and reuse the key for every host you register.

In this step, you create an activation key that points to the Dev environment and your content view.

.Prerequisites
* You have published and promoted a content view to the Dev lifecycle environment.

.Procedure
. In the {ProjectWebUI}, navigate to *Content* > *Lifecycle* > *Activation Keys*.
. Click *Create Activation Key*.
. In the *Name* field, enter a name, for example `my-dev-key`.
. Ensure the *Unlimited hosts* checkbox is selected.
. Optional: In the *Description* field, enter a description.
. On the *Content view environments* card, click *Assign content view environments*.
. Select the *Dev* lifecycle environment.
. Select the content view you created, for example *My Content View*.
. Click *Save* to save the content view environment.
. Click *Save* to create the activation key.

You now have an activation key that you can use to register hosts.
Each registered host will automatically receive content from the Dev lifecycle environment and your content view.
