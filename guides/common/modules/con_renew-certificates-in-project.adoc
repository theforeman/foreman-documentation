= Renew certificates in {Project}

[role="_abstract"]
When the SSL or CA certificates on {ProjectServer} are about to expire or have changed, you want to renew them on {ProjectServer} and refresh the CA certificate on your hosts, so you can keep the connections in your environment trusted and uninterrupted.
